Shaq Channels Arnold in Viral TikTok Workout

By ViralHoods Staff • October 13, 2026 • 2 min read

Photo: Unsplash (illustrative)

Shaquille O’Neal dropped a TikTok workout video that has the internet in stitches and in awe. The NBA legend channeled his inner Arnold Schwarzenegger, flexing, posing, and lifting with classic bodybuilding flair.

Fans immediately flooded the comments with jokes, memes, and comparisons to the Terminator-era Arnold. The clip has racked up millions of views, proving that Shaq’s larger-than-life personality still dominates social media years after retirement.

Whether it’s basketball, comedy, or now fitness content, Shaq continues to be one of the most reliably viral celebrities on the planet.
