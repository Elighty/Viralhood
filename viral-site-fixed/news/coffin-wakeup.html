Teen Wakes Up in Coffin at Her Own Funeral in Guatemala

By ViralHoods Staff • October 13, 2026 • 2 min read

Photo: Unsplash (illustrative)

A video from a funeral in Guatemala has shocked and captivated millions after a 15-year-old girl, presumed dead, suddenly woke up inside her coffin during the ceremony.

Mourners can be heard screaming and rushing forward as the teenager opens her eyes and begins to move. The clip spread rapidly across platforms, with people expressing everything from relief and joy to disbelief and fear.

Local reports say the girl is now recovering and under medical care. The incident has sparked discussions about medical errors, funeral practices, and the power of viral video to turn private moments into global events.

It’s one of those rare viral stories that feels both unbelievable and deeply human.
