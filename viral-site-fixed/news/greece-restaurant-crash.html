Car Smashes into Packed Restaurant in Greece

By ViralHoods Staff • October 12, 2026 • 2 min read

Photo: Unsplash (illustrative)

Wild video from a popular restaurant in Greece shows a car smashing through the wall while diners were eating. Witnesses described the scene as “like watching a movie.”

The alleged drunk driver lost control and plowed into the packed establishment. Thankfully, early reports indicate no fatalities, though several people were injured and the restaurant sustained major damage.

The footage spread instantly across social media, with people sharing the clip alongside messages of relief that it wasn’t worse. It’s another reminder of how quickly real-world incidents become global viral moments.

Sources: Local Greek media, viral video reports
