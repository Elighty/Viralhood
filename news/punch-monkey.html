Punch the Baby Monkey and His IKEA Orangutan Steal Hearts Worldwide

By ViralHoods Staff • October 14, 2026 • 2 min read

Photo: Unsplash (illustrative)

In a zoo in Japan, a baby Japanese macaque named Punch was rejected by his mother and struggled to join the troop. What happened next became one of 2026’s most heartwarming viral stories.

Punch found comfort in dragging around an IKEA orangutan plush toy. Videos of the little monkey clutching his “friend” and navigating the enclosure have exploded across TikTok, Instagram, and X, with millions of views and endless comments about how relatable and pure the bond is.

Animal lovers worldwide have shared the clips, turning Punch into an internet sensation. Some have even started petitions or sent messages of support to the zoo. It’s a simple story of an animal finding comfort, and the internet has embraced it completely.

Punch’s story is a reminder that viral content doesn’t always need drama — sometimes a baby monkey and a stuffed animal are enough to capture the world’s attention.
