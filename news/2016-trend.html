2026 Is the New 2016: Nostalgia Trend Takes Over Social Media

By ViralHoods Staff • October 12, 2026 • 2 min read

Photo: Unsplash (illustrative)

A massive nostalgia wave has taken over social media with the phrase “2026 is the new 2016.” Users are digging up old filters, recreating the Mannequin Challenge, and posting Pokémon Go memories.

The trend started gaining steam late in 2025 and exploded in early 2026 as Gen Z reminisced about a time many of them remember as the peak of internet culture. Snapchat puppy filters, dabbing, and bottle flips are all making comebacks in videos and carousels.

Whether it’s genuine longing or ironic fun, the 2016 revival shows how quickly the internet can turn the past into the present’s biggest trend.
